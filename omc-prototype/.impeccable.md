## Design Context

### Users
Software Engineers, QA Engineers, Product Managers. They use it while developing, testing, and reviewing AI agents. They are focused on debugging, analyzing plans, and evaluating agent performance.

### Brand Personality
Professional, Transparent, Precise. It should evoke confidence, clarity, and control.

### Aesthetic Direction
Technical, Information-dense, Structured. It should prioritize readability over playfulness. No dark mode required for now. WCAG AA compliance.

### Design Principles
1. Clarity Over Decoration: Focus on presenting dense technical information clearly rather than adding visual flair.
2. Distinct Information Hierarchy: Use typography (Avenir Next, SFMono, Condensed headers) and surface layering (var(--prototype-surface), var(--prototype-surface-strong)) to separate code, metadata, and structural elements.
3. Purposeful Accents: Reserve color (var(--prototype-accent), var(--prototype-warning), var(--prototype-success)) strictly for states, actions, and status indicators.
